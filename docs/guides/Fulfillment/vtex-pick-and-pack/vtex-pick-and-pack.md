[VTEX Pick and Pack](https://help.vtex.com/en/tutorial/vtex-pick-and-pack--1OOops3WrUyz7e0bnhkfXU) is the solution that enables merchants to manage order picking, packing, and delivery from physical stores and warehouses to customers’ addresses or pickup points. All management is made in one place, providing visibility and control for merchants through the whole fulfillment process.

VTEX Pick and Pack is a solution composed of:

- **[Orders](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-pick-and-pack-orders):** Admin page to consult, search, and filter the orders processed by the solution.
- **[Worksheets](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-pick-and-pack-worksheets):** Admin page to create and follow up on the worksheets that group orders for picking.
- **[Insights](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-pick-and-pack-insights):** Admin page with process time and performance indicators for the fulfillment operation.
- **[Settings](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-pick-and-pack-settings):** Admin page to configure orders, worksheets, items, automation, users, and integrations.
- **[Last Mile](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-pick-and-pack-last-mile):** Admin page to track deliveries made by integrated carriers, with shipping status updates, and to manage the in-store pickup flow. The Last Mile mobile app for drivers was discontinued in 2024.
- **[Mobile app](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/vtex-pick-and-pack-mobile):** Application meant exclusively for pickers, so that they can perform their tasks with no access to the store’s Admin. The app is available for Android devices only.

> Learn more about developer capabilities allowed for VTEX Pick and Pack: [VTEX Pick and Pack Get Order Status and Information API](https://developers.vtex.com/docs/guides/vtex-pick-and-pack-get-order-status-and-information-api)
