---
title: "Keeping split cart lines"
---

By default, FastStore merges cart lines that share the same SKU, seller, attachments, and services into a single line. This is the right behavior for most stores. However, some stores need each unit of a product to remain a separate line in the cart, for example, when shoppers choose different [assembly options](https://help.vtex.com/en/docs/tutorials/assembly-options) (such as installation or extended warranty) for each unit, or when each unit must be handled as its own line in an ERP integration.

The `preserveCartLines` flag makes FastStore respect the lines that your store split in Checkout, instead of merging them again.

> ℹ️ FastStore doesn't split lines on its own. Adding a product with quantity `2` still creates a single line, with or without `preserveCartLines`. The split must be done in Checkout.

## Default behavior and behavior with the flag

Consider a shopper who buys 2 units of the same refrigerator. Before the shopper chooses services for each unit, your store splits the line in Checkout using the [split item endpoint](https://developers.vtex.com/docs/api-reference/checkout-api#post-/api/checkout/pub/orderForm/-orderFormId-/items/-itemUniqueId-/split), resulting in `[refrigerator x1, refrigerator x1]`.

| | `preserveCartLines: false` (default) | `preserveCartLines: true` |
|---|---|---|
| **Minicart** | Shows one line with quantity `2`. | Shows two lines with quantity `1` each. |
| **Checkout** | On the next cart validation, FastStore sends the merged quantity, and Checkout undoes the split. | The split is kept after the cart is validated and the page is reloaded. |
| **Editing or removing a line** | Because lines share the same ID, the action may affect the wrong line. | The action affects only the selected line. |

The behavior of stores that don't enable the flag remains unchanged.

## Before you begin

- Update your store to FastStore `4.10.3` or a later version. To do so, follow [Updating the CLI package version](https://developers.vtex.com/docs/guides/faststore/developer-tools-updating-the-cli-package-version).
- Make sure your store splits items in Checkout. FastStore only preserves lines that already exist as separate lines in the Order Form.

## Instructions

### Step 1 - Enable `preserveCartLines`

1. Open your `discovery.config.js` file.
2. In the `api` object, set `preserveCartLines` to `true`.

```js discovery.config.js
...
api: {
  ...
  preserveCartLines: true,
}
```

### Step 2 - Validate the cart locally

Before deploying, test the flag locally, as it changes how cart lines are matched with Checkout.

In the terminal, run:

```sh
yarn dev
```

### Step 3 - Check the cart behavior

With the local server running, create a cart with two units of the same SKU, split it in Checkout, and confirm the following:

1. Call `POST /api/checkout/pub/orderForm/{orderFormId}/items/{itemUniqueId}/split` with a body that divides the item into lines of quantity `1`, for example, `[{"quantity": 1}, {"quantity": 1}]`.
2. Open your store with the cart's `checkout.vtex.com` cookie and open the minicart. It should show two lines, and Checkout should keep two lines after you reload the page.
3. Increase the quantity of one line. Only that line changes in Checkout.
4. Remove one line. Only that line is removed, and its services (if any) stay on the correct unit.

If your store uses assembly options, also confirm that the cart stays stable when the same SKU appears with and without a service, and that the minicart matches the Checkout cart line by line.

If you find any issue, contact [VTEX Support](https://supporticket.vtex.com/support) with the `orderFormId` of the affected cart.

### Step 4 - Deploy the changes

1. Commit the changes to your `discovery.config.js` file.
2. Push the changes to the main branch of your repository.

This triggers a [WebOps](https://developers.vtex.com/docs/guides/faststore/webops-dashboard) build. Once it completes, the `preserveCartLines` flag will be active in your live store.

## Things to consider

- **Assembly options and VTEX Services**: assembly options are added to the cart as child items of a product line and are handled by this flag. Services stored in `bundleItems` follow a different mechanism and don't require `preserveCartLines`.
- **Promotion splits**: Checkout may also return several lines of the same SKU because of promotions. Because of this, the flag is opt-in. Without it, FastStore keeps merging those lines.
- **Minicart UI**: the default minicart doesn't display assembly options for each line. The split lines follow the Checkout behavior, but you may need a custom component to present the services chosen for each unit.
- **Services hidden from search**: assembly option SKUs can have **Display on website** turned off. FastStore keeps the cart working in this scenario. Learn more in [this release note](https://developers.vtex.com/updates/release-notes/2026-10-30-faststore-cart-validation-hidden-assembly-skus).
